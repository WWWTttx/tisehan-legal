Tisehan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026 · Terms of Use

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tisehan handles information. The App does not create an account and does not post to a live chat network. Most information stays on your device.

1. Summary

Tisehan stores scene drafts, media copies, generated reels, StoreKit transaction identifiers, and display preferences in the App sandbox. Apple processes in-app purchases. This version does not track you for advertising and does not send notifications.

2. Information Stored on This Device

Message text, reactions, scene type, Sample persona choice, theme, view-count mode, photo and video working copies, voice notes, generated scene reels, purchase transaction IDs, an internal generation ledger, and Light/Dark preference stay on this device. There is no Tisehan login, email, phone number, or username.

3. No Account Data

Tisehan does not ask for email, phone number, username, or password. Erase All App Data deletes in-app records on this device. There is no cloud profile to delete.

4. Permissions

Camera is used only after you tap Capture, to attach a photo or video with in-app filters to the current Sample message. Photos is used only after you tap Import or Save to Photos, to choose existing media or save a finished scene still. Microphone is used only after you tap Record Voice Note. App Tracking Transparency and notification dialogs appear the first time you open Character Studio. Those prompts are display-only in this version: Tisehan does not read the advertising identifier for tracking and does not send local or remote notifications.

5. Purchases

Gift packs and treasure chests are Consumable products processed by Apple. Apple may collect payment information under Apple’s privacy policy. Tisehan stores verified transaction identifiers and an on-device generation entitlement. Entitlements cannot be restored after the App is removed or after Erase All App Data.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. This version does not upload Your Content to a live community. Fandom Sample people are scripted and stored with the App. Local report and block choices stay on this device.

7. Retention and Deletion

Data remains until you delete a scene reel, use Erase All App Data, or remove the App. Erase All App Data permanently removes projects, media, Sample history, report and block lists, and unused gift packs or treasure chests.

8. Children

Tisehan is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your region) and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Support link on the Tisehan App Store listing when available, or contact the developer who distributed this build.

Tisehan Support

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Tisehan is a private conversation rehearsal app. Drafts, media, gift packs, and scene reels stay on this device. This version does not create an account and does not send live messages or calls.

How to reach us

Use the Support link on the Tisehan App Store listing when available, or contact the developer who distributed this build.

Purchases

Gift packs and treasure chests are Consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. Refunds and billing questions for Apple purchases are handled under Apple’s policies. Entitlements cannot be restored after the App is removed or after Delete Account / Erase All App Data.

Content safety

In Fandom you can filter Sample people, report entries, or block people. Report and block choices stay on this device in this version.
